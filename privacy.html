Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What EduGod stores

EduGod stores equation history, practice results, review schedules, and preferences in your browser's local storage. Documents you import into the Library are stored in that browser's IndexedDB. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to EduGod servers. You can export progress as JSON, download individual documents, or use the in-app delete option to remove stored data. Clearing your browser's site data also removes it.

Hosting and optional speech

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with serving the site under its own privacy policy. If you choose browser voice input, your browser or device provider may process speech; availability and processing vary by browser. You can use EduGod without voice input.

Advertising

EduGod does not currently display third-party ads or run ad tracking. If advertising is introduced, this policy will be updated before ads are enabled to explain the provider, cookies or other identifiers, and available choices.

Contact

For a privacy question, open an issue in the EduGod repository. Do not post personal information in a public issue.
